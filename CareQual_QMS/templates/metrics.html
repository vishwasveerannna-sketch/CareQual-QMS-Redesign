    How these numbers are calculated

    The same definitions are used on the Overview and Release pages.

    • Test pass rate · Passed ÷ (Passed + Failed + Blocked). Tests that are Not Run are excluded.
    • Review completion · reviews with any decision other than Pending ÷ all reviews.
    • Open defects · every defect whose status is not Closed. This includes Rejected defects until they are verified and closed.
    • Open CAPA · every action not yet Closed. Past due needs a due date earlier than today.
    • Requirements approved · {{ s.req_approved }} of {{ s.req_total }} ({{ s.req_rate }}%). Critical requirements feed a release gate.
